# 30-Day Recovery Calendar: 16 Oct – 14 Nov 2026

## Principles
- **Keep the live-class schedule exactly as the batch needs it.** This calendar only **adds** YouTube-specific items:
  - Shorts: 1 a day, cut from the previous day's class (≈5 min of editing each).
  - Chapter assets: a one-shot, PYQ or test when a chapter finishes.
  - Community posts: 2–3 a week.
- **Production load added per week:** ~7 Shorts (editor: ~1 h total), 1 one-shot or PYQ (faculty: 2 h live, recorded as a normal live class), 3 community posts.
- **No empty days.** If a class is cancelled, publish the backup listed for that week.
- Lecture numbers and sub-topics after your supplied schedule are written `{from schedule}`. I haven't invented them.
- **Publishing times:** keep the current class times (08:30 / 12:00 / 19:00 IST in your schedule). Analytics shows weekends are weakest, but weekend uploads are also fewer, so the cause isn't proven. Week 3 tests Shorts at 17:00 vs 21:00. Check Studio's "When your viewers are on YouTube" to refine.
- **Format of each row:** content | format | title | thumbnail | supporting item | metric | reason.
