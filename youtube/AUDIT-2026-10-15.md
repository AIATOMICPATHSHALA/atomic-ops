## 1. Executive summary

Atomic Pathshala is a **six-month-old channel** (created 15 Apr 2026; first views 8 Jul 2026). It has **458 subscribers** and 157 uploads. Almost all of those uploads are 1–2 hour live-class recordings of the *Selection Pro Batch* (NEET 2027, Hindi medium).

**The channel was never "growing and then declining" in the usual sense.** Engaged views have been flat to slowly drifting down since late July (≈300–340/day in late July, ≈200–250/day in September). They then fell sharply in the week of 5–11 Oct (**108/day, −56% vs the previous week**). Net subscribers have also stalled: **+6 between 1 and 12 Oct**, compared with +223 in July.

The evidence points to **one structural problem with three visible symptoms**:

1. **The channel only reaches people who are already subscribed.** 78% of views in the last 90 days came from Browse features (home and subscription feeds), and new uploads drive that traffic. YouTube Search brought 3% of views, and the top search term was the brand name itself. So views rise and fall with the number of uploads, and they fell when uploads dropped from 11 to 7 a week.
2. **Non-subscribers leave in the first 1–2 minutes.** 65% of views come from non-subscribers, but they watch for only **2 min 5 s on average (2.8% of the video)**. The transcript of IUPAC Lec 13 shows why: about 4 minutes of mic checks, greetings and attendance, then homework checking, then a personal story around minute 9–10, before any new teaching.
3. **The packaging is built for enrolled batch students, not for searchers or browsers.** Titles lead with batch and brand names, lecture numbering is inconsistent, some descriptions and tags are copy-paste leftovers (including literal placeholder tags "ChapterName" and "TopicName"), broken stub recordings appear in the feed, and there are almost no Shorts or chapter-level one-shots, which are the formats competitors use for discovery.

**What will move the numbers (summary of the roadmap in §8):**

- (a) Start every recording with teaching, or trim and add chapter markers to the replay.
- (b) Clean up the broken uploads and the metadata defects.
- (c) Add search-shaped content on top of the batch lectures: one chapter one-shot or PYQ session per subject per chapter, plus 1–2 Shorts a day cut from the lectures.
- (d) Keep the upload schedule steady so the subscriber feed does not dry up.

None of this requires deleting, hiding or bulk-editing existing videos.

## 6. Title, thumbnail, description, tag and playlist system

### 6.1 Title formula (lecture recordings)

```
{Chapter EN} ({Chapter HI}) L{NN}: {Sub-topic} | Class {11/12} {Subject} | NEET 2027 | {Faculty}
```

Example (only if accurate): `Isomerism (समावयवता) L09: Geometrical Isomerism | Class 11 Chemistry | NEET 2027 | Firoz Sir`

Rules:

- Put the **sub-topic in the first 60 characters**. That's what shows on mobile and what Suggested matches on.
- Always include **Class 11/12**. It is in the highest-volume search phrasing for every chapter checked.
- **Drop "Selection Pro Batch" and "Atomic Pathshala" from titles.** The channel name already shows under every video; move the batch name to the description.
- Use one style for lecture numbers: `L01`…`L99`. Not "Lec :", "Lecture", "L-" and "L :" mixed.
- Use one hashtag in the title at most, or none (hashtags go in the description).
- One-shot, PYQ and test videos lead with the format: `Isomerism One Shot | Class 11 Chemistry | NEET 2027 PYQs Included | Firoz Sir`.

### 6.2 Thumbnail system

Each template is the same 1280×720 layout with a different colour band:

| Format | Band colour | Big text (≤4 words) | Small text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lecture | Subject colour: Chemistry teal, Physics blue, Biology green | Sub-topic (e.g. "GEOMETRICAL ISOMERS") | "L09 • Class 11" | Faculty cut-out right third; one diagram or structure left |
| One-shot | Orange | "ISOMERISM ONE SHOT" | "Full chapter • PYQs" | Faculty + timer icon |
| PYQ | Red | "ISOMERISM PYQs" | "NEET 2015–2026" (only if true) | Faculty + "?" icon |
| Chapter test / discussion | Purple | "CHAPTER TEST" / "TEST SOLUTIONS" | Date | Question-paper graphic |
| Short | — | Hook question in Hindi | — | Vertical; face + one diagram |

Rules: ≤4 words of large text, readable at 160 px wide; faculty face visible; never more than 2 colours plus white; the AP logo small in one fixed corner. **Don't replace existing thumbnails in bulk.** Start with the 10 videos in `proposed-metadata-changes.md`, and only once the Studio CTR export shows which ones underperform.

### 6.3 Description template

See [`templates/lecture-publishing-template.md`](templates/lecture-publishing-template.md). Order:

1. One-line hook in Hindi or English (what the student can do after watching).
2. Sub-topics covered, **from the teacher's lesson plan**.
3. ⏱ Chapters (only real timestamps).
4. ⏮ Previous lecture / ⏭ Next lecture / 📚 Chapter playlist links.
5. Batch, app, website and Telegram links.
6. Subscribe line.
7. 3–5 hashtags.

### 6.4 Tags

- 10–15 tags. Exact chapter name (EN + HI), "{chapter} class 11/12", "{chapter} neet", one sub-topic tag, the faculty name, "atomic pathshala".
- Remove boilerplate board lists (UP/MP/Bihar…) unless the lecture is board-specific.
- Remove "jee" tags on NEET Hindi-medium content.
- Tags matter **least** of all the metadata. YouTube says they mostly help with misspellings. Fix the errors for hygiene, but don't expect views from it.

### 6.5 Playlists

One playlist per chapter (lecture order), plus cross-cutting lists: "Chapter Tests", "PYQ Sessions", "One Shots", and "Class 12 Biology" / "Class 11 Biology". Every new upload goes into its chapter playlist **at publish time**. Playlist engaged views fell 57% in the last 30 days.
